COMPLAINT REPORT — AMOXICILLIN TRIHYDRATE API

Complaint received: 12 January 2026, via Email.
Customer: Medlife Pharmaceuticals Ltd., Mr. Rajesh Kumar (quality@medlifepharma.com), India.

During incoming inspection of Amoxicillin Trihydrate API (Compacted, bulk density
0.55 g/ml), our QC laboratory observed a yellowish discoloration in 3 of 10
polyethylene bags from batch AMX-250814 (manufacturing date 14 August 2025,
retest date 31 July 2027). The material specification requires a white to
off-white powder.

Approximately 120 kg of the total 1000 kg supplied quantity is affected. The
bags were stored at 25°C/60% RH as per label instructions. A sample from
discolored bag #7 has been retained for analysis. No finished product has been
manufactured from this material and no product has reached the market.

We request a full investigation, including a review of the stability data for
this batch and confirmation of the storage conditions maintained during shipment.
