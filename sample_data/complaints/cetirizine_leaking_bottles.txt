CUSTOMER COMPLAINT SUMMARY

Distributor: Pharmanova Trading GmbH (Germany), Mr. Lukas Weber
(lk.weber@pharmanova.de). Complaint received: 20 January 2026, via Letter.

Product: Cetirizine Hydrochloride Oral Solution 5 mg/5 ml, 100 ml PET bottles
(Finished Dosage Form). Batch CTZ-250917, manufactured 17 September 2025,
expiry 16 September 2027.

Complaint description: During routine shelf replenishment, 48 of 240 bottles
were found leaking from the cap area, with sticky residue visible on bottle
exteriors and inside shipping cartons. All affected bottles originate from a
single pallet shipped in December 2025. No patient exposure or adverse event
has been reported. Retail stock has been isolated pending instructions.

Requested action: replacement of affected stock and an investigation of the
cap liner sealing process.
