URGENT QUALITY COMPLAINT — LABEL MIX-UP

Complaint received: 3 February 2026, via Phone, followed by written confirmation.
Reported by: Northside Pharmacy Group (distributor), attention Ms. Sarah Williams
(complaints@northsidepharmacy.co.uk), United Kingdom.

Two retail customers returned Ibuprofen 200 mg Soft Gel Capsules cartons
(batch IBU-251022, manufactured 22 October 2025, expiry 31 October 2028) in
which the outer carton states 200 mg while the inner blister foil is printed
400 mg. Approximately 60 packs from a single pharmacy shipment of 600 packs
have been identified so far.

Remaining stock has been quarantined at all branches as a precaution. A patient
with a history of gastric ulcer purchased one affected pack; no adverse event
has been reported to date, but the potential for double-dose intake exists.

We request an immediate investigation and notification of the MHRA if required.
